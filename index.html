Welcome to Checkpoint Courier!

                


                

                    

                        
You're a courier trying to make deliveries in a region with...
                        creative bureaucracy and checkpoint enforcement.


                        
How to Play:

                        
    
                            
  • 📦 Select deliveries - Higher risk = higher reward
    • 
                            
  • 📋 Get permits - Required for legal passage
    • 
                            
  • 🚚 Drive carefully - Speed affects checkpoint outcomes
    • 
                            
  • 🛑 Handle checkpoints - Officers have different personalities
    • 
                            
  • 🔧 Upgrade your truck - Better equipment, better outcomes
    • 
                        


                        
At Checkpoints:

                        
You'll encounter officers with different attitudes. You can show
                        your permit, pay fines, negotiate... or try other approaches.
                        Choose wisely - your reputation follows you.
